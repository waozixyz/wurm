Your journey stays with you.

Updated 27 October 2026.

Apfelwurm stores your run, preferences and edited levels on this device. Every level can be unlocked by playing, without an account or payment. There are no ads or analytics.

Optional accounts

Profile uses the same Entity account provider as Shovey. You can create an account, import your account key or link another device. Private keys stay in the host's private storage. Keep exported keys and recovery codes private.

When signed in, completion marks, active playtime, website-visit unlocks, the selected skin and preferences are synced through your selected node. Account records are encrypted by the host. An unfinished run and editor drafts remain on this device. Each account's game records stay separate.

Dew and skins

Three optional skins cost 10, 20 or 30 Dew. Purchases require sign-in and confirmation. The Waozi node processes the account, product, price and an idempotent receipt key so retrying cannot charge twice. Purchased ownership is restored from that account's Dew ledger. You can also earn skins by playing for five hours, reaching level 40 or opening waozi.xyz. The website unlock records that you opened the link; it does not track your browsing.

In the Google Play version, Dew is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the Waozi node receives the order number, the Dew pack and Google's purchase token to confirm the purchase and add the Dew to your account. Waozi never receives your payment details. The direct download and F-Droid versions buy Dew with Monero at dew.waozi.xyz.

The default node is api.waozi.xyz. It receives the connection and account information needed for authentication, synchronization and Dew. Another node has its own operator and policies. Offline play does not require a network connection.

Your data

Profile offers key backup, device linking, logout and remote-data management on supported hosts. Logging out does not delete remote records. Clearing this site's browser data, or uninstalling the Android app, removes local saves and keys. Back up your account key first.

Delete your account

You can request deletion of your account and the data Waozi's node holds for it without reinstalling Apfelwurm. Email waozi@proton.me with the subject Apfelwurm account deletion, your public account ID and the node address. We verify that you control the account before acting. Never send your private key or recovery code. For another node, contact its operator. Deletion does not erase local saves or exported files on your devices.

Cloudflare hosts this site and receives ordinary connection information when you load it.
