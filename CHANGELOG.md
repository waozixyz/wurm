# Changelog

## [0.1.2] - 2026-10-25

- Travel through all 66 levels from Meadow through deciduous Leafwood, coniferous Pinewood and Lake to the Mountains, with distinct terrain and challenges throughout.
- Turn as your finger moves while touching the board, and make successive corners without lifting.
- Browse and edit every landscape while keeping existing personal level edits.
- Hear continuous acoustic music across landscape changes, with smoother looping and playback on slower phones.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-25

- Download Apfelwurm separately from Arcade and play the same Garden and Forest campaign.
- Keep portable level-editor changes in the game's private saves and return to Arcade from the title.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-25

- Play 66 distinct Garden and Forest challenges, with five apples in the opening levels and a gentler level 2.
- Meet a friendly orange worm in a detailed garden title, with one large Play button and artwork composed for portrait and landscape.
- Follow a slimmer worm with a mouth that faces its movement, continuous forest walls, clear solid obstacles and stone gate posts.
- See remaining lives as hearts, with game over stopping at zero lives.
- Use large touch controls and readable pause, game-over and completion cards on phones.
- Download signed Android APKs for both ARM architectures, with standard and F-Droid-style filenames.
