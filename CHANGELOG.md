# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-25

- Play 66 distinct Garden and Forest challenges, with five apples in the opening levels and a gentler level 2.
- Meet a friendly orange worm in a detailed garden title, with one large Play button and artwork composed for portrait and landscape.
- Follow a slimmer worm with a mouth that faces its movement, continuous forest walls, clear solid obstacles and stone gate posts.
- See remaining lives as hearts, with game over stopping at zero lives.
- Use large touch controls and readable pause, game-over and completion cards on phones.
- Download signed Android APKs for both ARM architectures, with standard and F-Droid-style filenames.
